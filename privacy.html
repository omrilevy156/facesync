Privacy Policy

Effective November 6, 2026

FaceSync: Glow Up ("FaceSync", "we") helps you build a personal grooming and glow-up routine. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, where it goes, how long it's kept, and how to delete it. In short: no account, no email, no ads, no selling data; your photos are deleted right after the analysis.

1. No account needed

When you first use online features, the app creates an anonymous ID for your phone. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.

2. Face Data and TrueDepth (Face ID camera)

The face scan is optional and for ages 16 and up. It only runs after you agree on the consent screen. You can skip it and use your answers instead; you still get a look and a routine.

What face data is collected

  • Your two photos (front and side).
  • TrueDepth / ARKit face tracking, on iPhones with Face ID, while you frame the photos: head angles (turn, tilt, roll), distance from the camera, face position and face mesh, gaze direction, and a few expression values (such as blinking, smiling or an open mouth).
  • Face landmarks, computed on your phone by Apple Vision and MediaPipe from the camera images and the photos: face points, head angles and the outline of the side profile. On devices without TrueDepth, these guide the framing instead.

What it is used for

  • Guiding you to frame the photos (distance, head turn, a steady and well-lit frame).
  • Describing visible features (for example face shape, hair texture, skin tone), which build your plan.

TrueDepth and ARKit data stays on your device

TrueDepth and ARKit face data is processed only on your phone, in real time, and is discarded as you go. It is never stored, never sent to us or anyone else, and never shared. The app does not save depth maps or face meshes. On iPhones with Face ID, your two color photos come from the same camera session, but only the color image is sent; no depth data, mesh or tracking values go with it.

Your two photos

  • They are sent over HTTPS to our server, which passes them to our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude), only to generate the description of your features. The scan never tries to identify who you are.
  • We don't store them. Our server keeps them in memory only for that one request.
  • No user ID is attached to the photos sent to Anthropic. Our server uses your anonymous ID only to apply the daily scan limit.
  • Your phone deletes its copies when the scan is done (or after the retake, if the scan asks for one). A day-1 or progress photo is a separate choice, and stays on your phone (section 3).
  • Anthropic's retention: under Anthropic's published terms for its commercial API (checked November 6, 2026), inputs and outputs are automatically deleted within 30 days of receipt or generation. If its automated systems flag a request under its Usage Policy, Anthropic may keep the inputs and outputs for up to 2 years and safety classification scores for up to 7 years, and it may keep data longer where the law requires. Its Commercial Terms say it may not train models on this content. See Anthropic's retention policy.

What is kept, and where

The description that comes back, and measurements your phone computes from the face landmarks (normalized face points, the side-profile curve, and labels and estimates such as eye color; no image), are stored only on your phone. They are never sent to our server. Settings → Delete my data deletes them, and deleting the app also removes them. We keep no face data on our server, so there is nothing to retain there.

What face data is never used for

Face data is never used for advertising, marketing, tracking, identifying you, or data mining. It is never sold, and it isn't shared with anyone other than Anthropic as described above (the two photos only).

3. Progress photos stay on your phone

If you choose to save progress photos (to compare with day 1), they are kept only on your phone. They are not uploaded.

4. What stays on your phone

Your quiz answers, your plan, your routine progress, reminders, progress photos and app settings are stored on your phone only.

5. What our server keeps

Our server (Supabase, hosted in the EU, Frankfurt) keeps only what the app needs to work fairly and safely:

  • the time of each photo scan, with your anonymous ID (to limit how many scans can be made per day);
  • for abuse protection only: a scrambled code (a one-way hash) of a random ID this app keeps on your phone and of your network address, with the time of each scan. They aren't linked to your anonymous ID, can't be turned back into the ID or the address, and are deleted after 48 hours;
  • your Glow Points, the days you completed (for streaks) and the cosmetic items you unlocked in the in-app shop, with your anonymous ID.

No photos, no quiz answers and no feature descriptions are stored on the server.

6. Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple through your Apple ID; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. It receives your anonymous ID and your App Store purchase information (for example the plan, renewal dates and trial status).

7. Crash reports

This version of the app doesn't send crash reports or analytics to us or to any third party. If you agree to share diagnostics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may give us anonymous crash logs.

8. Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone (local notifications). No notification data is sent to us.

9. What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your data for marketing.
  • We never give you an attractiveness score or rating.

10. How long we keep data, and deleting it

  • Photos and face data: not kept by us (see section 2).
  • Server data (section 5): kept until you delete it, except the abuse-protection codes, which are deleted after 48 hours.
  • Delete everything anytime: in the app, open Settings → Delete my data. This permanently deletes your anonymous user and all its data on our server, and erases the app's data on your phone. Deleting the app also removes what it stores on the phone. The one exception is the random ID used only for the scan limit (section 5): it stays in the phone's secure storage (Keychain) so that the limit can't be reset by reinstalling. It contains nothing about you or your face.
  • Your App Store subscription is managed by Apple: cancel it in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. RevenueCat keeps purchase records as needed for Apple's receipts.

11. Age

FaceSync is for ages 13 and up. The photo scan is for ages 16 and up. Diet and supplement guidance is only offered to adults (18+). We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13.

12. Not medical advice

FaceSync gives general grooming and lifestyle information. It is not medical advice and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. See the Terms of Use.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its use. Most data is only on your phone, and you can delete the rest yourself in the app. For anything else, contact us.

14. Service providers

  • Supabase (server and database, EU)
  • Anthropic (AI description of your two photos, only with your consent; see section 2)
  • RevenueCat (subscription status)
  • Apple (App Store payments and notifications)

15. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app when the change is important.
