Privacy Policy

Effective October 21, 2026

FaceSync: Glow Up ("FaceSync", "we") helps you build a personal grooming and glow-up routine. This policy explains what data the app uses, why, where it goes, how long it's kept, and how to delete it. In short: no account, no email, no ads, no selling data; your photos are deleted right after the analysis.

1. No account needed

When you first use online features, the app creates an anonymous ID for your phone. We don't ask for your name, email or phone number.

2. Your face photos (optional, ages 16 and up)

  • Consent first. The photo scan only runs after you agree on the consent screen and confirm you're 16 or older. You can skip it and use your answers instead; you still get a look and a routine.
  • What happens: your two photos (front and side) are sent securely to our server, which passes them to our AI provider, Anthropic (Claude), only to describe your facial features (for example face shape, hair texture, skin tone). The scan never tries to identify who you are.
  • Not stored. Our server doesn't save your photos; they are discarded as soon as the analysis is done. Anthropic doesn't use them to train its models and deletes them within 30 days under its API terms. The phone deletes its copies when the scan is done.
  • The feature description that comes back is saved on your phone, to build your plan.

3. Face data from your camera (ARKit) stays on your phone

To guide you while taking the photos (distance, head turn, a steady frame), the app uses on-device face tracking: Apple's ARKit / TrueDepth face data and on-device face landmarks. This data is processed only on your phone, in real time. It is never stored, never sent to us or anyone else, and never used for advertising, marketing or data mining.

4. Progress photos stay on your phone

If you choose to save progress photos (to compare with day 1), they are kept only on your phone. They are not uploaded.

5. What stays on your phone

Your quiz answers, your plan, your routine progress, reminders, progress photos and app settings are stored on your phone only.

6. What our server keeps

Our server (Supabase, hosted in the EU, Frankfurt) keeps only what the app needs to work fairly and safely, linked to your anonymous ID:

  • the time of each photo scan (to limit how many scans can be made per day);
  • your Glow Points, the days you completed (for streaks) and the cosmetic items you unlocked in the in-app shop.

No photos, no quiz answers and no feature descriptions are stored on the server.

7. Subscriptions

Payments are handled by Apple through your Apple ID; we never see your card details. We use RevenueCat to check whether your subscription is active. It receives your anonymous ID and your App Store purchase information (for example the plan, renewal dates and trial status).

8. Crash reports

If the app crashes, we receive a technical crash report through Sentry (device model, iOS version, app version and what went wrong) so we can fix it. Crash reports contain no photos, no screenshots and no personal details.

9. Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your phone (local notifications). No notification data is sent to us.

10. What we don't do

  • No advertising, no ad tracking, no tracking across other apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your data for marketing.
  • We never give you an attractiveness score or rating.

11. How long we keep data, and deleting it

  • Photos: not kept by us (see section 2).
  • Server data (section 6): kept until you delete it.
  • Crash reports: kept by Sentry for up to 90 days.
  • Delete everything anytime: in the app, open Settings → Delete my data. This permanently deletes your anonymous user and all its data on our server, and erases the app's data on your phone. Deleting the app also removes everything stored on the phone.
  • Your App Store subscription is managed by Apple: cancel it in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. RevenueCat keeps purchase records as needed for Apple's receipts.

12. Age

FaceSync is for ages 13 and up. The photo scan is for ages 16 and up. Diet and supplement guidance is only offered to adults (18+). We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13.

13. Not medical advice

FaceSync gives general grooming and lifestyle information. It is not medical advice and doesn't diagnose or treat any condition. See the Terms of Use.

14. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, or to object to its use. Most data is only on your phone, and you can delete the rest yourself in the app. For anything else, contact us.

15. Service providers

  • Supabase (server and database, EU)
  • Anthropic (AI analysis of your photos, only with your consent)
  • RevenueCat (subscription status)
  • Sentry (crash reports)
  • Apple (App Store payments and notifications)

16. Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above, and tell you in the app when the change is important.
