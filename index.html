Common questions

How do I cancel my subscription?

On your iPhone: Settings → your name (Apple ID) → Subscriptions → FaceSync → Cancel. You keep access until the end of the period you paid for.

I bought a plan but the app doesn't show it

In the app, open Settings → Restore purchases. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID you used to buy.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.

Are my photos stored?

No. Your scan photos are deleted right after the analysis, and progress photos stay only on your phone. See the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete my data?

In the app, open Settings → Delete my data. It erases everything, on your phone and on our server.

Is this medical advice?

No. FaceSync gives general grooming information. For skin conditions or health concerns, talk to a doctor or dermatologist.
